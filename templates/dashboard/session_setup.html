⚠
Keep your li_at cookie private — it grants full access to your LinkedIn account.
Never share it. It expires after ~1 year or when you log out of LinkedIn.
1
Open LinkedIn in your browser
Go to linkedin.com and make sure you're logged in. You should see your feed.
2
Open DevTools
Press the keyboard shortcut for your browser:
Chrome / Edge → F12 or ⌘ Cmd + Option + I (Mac)
Firefox → F12 or ⌘ Cmd + Option + I (Mac)
Safari → Enable DevTools in Settings first, then ⌘ Cmd + Option + I
3
Go to the Application tab → Cookies
In DevTools, click Application (Chrome/Edge) or Storage (Firefox) in the top tab bar. Then in the left sidebar expand Cookies and click https://www.linkedin.com.

4
Find and copy li_at
In the cookie list, click the row named exactly li_at. The full value appears at the bottom of DevTools (or in the Value column). It's a long string starting with AQE. Select all of it and copy.
↓ paste into the li_at field below
5
Find and copy JSESSIONID
Click the row named JSESSIONID. The value usually looks like ajax:XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX. Copy the full value including the ajax: prefix.
↓ paste into the JSESSIONID field below
6
Paste below and save
Fill in the form below and click Save Session.
